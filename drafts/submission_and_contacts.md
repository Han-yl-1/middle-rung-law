# Submission and outreach checklist (drafts only — nothing has been sent)

## arXiv
- Category: astro-ph.CO (cosmology) or gr-qc. First-time submitters need an endorsement from an established author in that category.
- Prepare: PDF + source (LaTeX or PDF-only accepted for some categories), abstract ≤1,920 characters, author list, license (CC-BY 4.0).

## Journals (after DESI final)
- Physical Review D; Monthly Notices of the RAS; JCAP. Requirements: LaTeX template, data-availability statement (link to GitHub + Zenodo), cover letter, suggested referees (optional).
- Gravity Research Foundation essay: deadline 31 March each year; ≤1,500 words; draft in 以太理论_征文/GRF_2027_essay_draft.md (活仓).
