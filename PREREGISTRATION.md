# OSF Preregistration — "Middle-Rung Law" predictions

**Title:** Predictions of a three-rung frequency ladder linking the Hubble rate, the Planck frequency and an "ether" frequency
**Date of preregistration:** 2026-11-05 (predictions fixed in a private git repository on that date; this text makes them public)

## 1. Hypothesis
The Planck frequency is the geometric mean of the cosmic expansion rate H and an ether frequency ν_e: ν_e · H = ν_P² at every epoch, equivalently ħG = c⁵/(4π² ν_e H). ν_e equals the de Broglie angular frequency of the Hubble sphere treated as one object (Kepler's third law with r = c/H, T = 2π/H, combined with Mc² = hν; normalization relation written by Zhang Xiangqian, 2026). The expansion follows standard early-universe physics and switches to coasting, H ∝ (1+z), near z ≈ 1.6–1.7.

Derived: N = ν_P/H = (1.44–1.47)×10⁶⁰; ν_e = (4.2–4.4)×10¹⁰² Hz; mid-rung frequency √(H ν_P) = 2.43–2.46 THz. Ether-flow (gravitational potential) changes add in quadrature; under current axioms a dual-optical-clock comparison next to any non-mass device should read zero.

## 3. Disclosure of data already examined
Data for P3 and P5 already existed. After fixing the predictions we compared them on 2026-11-05:
- P5: Ciocan et al. 2026 (Astronomy and Astrophysics 709, L16) find a₀ rising with z, 2.38×10⁻¹⁰ at z ≈ 1. Direction agrees; magnitude about 19% above our prediction. Earlier analyses (Milgrom 2017; Limbach et al. 2008) disfavour strong evolution.
- P3: DESI DR2 BAO (13 points). Best χ² = 27.7 for our model vs 11.5 for ΛCDM with free Ω_m and r_d. This is unfavourable to our model, with the largest residual at z = 0.934.
- P1: current GW-only constraints (±15–20) do not discriminate.
We will record every future comparison, favourable or not, without changing the predictions above.
