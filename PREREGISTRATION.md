## 1. Hypothesis
The Planck frequency is the geometric mean of the cosmic expansion rate H and an ether frequency ν_e: at every epoch t, ν_e(t) · H(t) = ν_P², equivalently ħG = c⁵/(4π² ν_e H). ν_e equals the de Broglie angular frequency of the Hubble sphere treated as one object (Kepler's third law with r = c/H, T = 2π/H, combined with Mc² = hν; normalization relation written by Zhang Xiangqian, 2026). Prior to cosmic age 3.912 Gyr (3.641–3.950 Gyr across H₀ = 61.8–63.5 km s⁻¹ Mpc⁻¹), the early universe undergoes hot radiation/matter evolution and intense hierarchical merging of smaller structural units; at **9.900 Gyr ago** (range 9.893–9.933 Gyr ago, redshift z_s = 1.686, range 1.669–1.817; acoustic-angle benchmark z_s = 1.721 gives age 3.837 Gyr and scale ratio 1+z_s = 2.721 ≈ e), large-scale galaxy structures stabilize and cosmic expansion locks into uniform coasting H(z) = H₀(1+z), after which ν_e(t) rises linearly with cosmic age while H(t) declines inversely with fixed product ν_P² = 8.715×10⁸⁴ Hz².

### 2.1 Operational Criteria and the Observational Collective Mechanism for P6 and P6.1
- **Effective mass m_eff:** The effective (motional) mass of the mode as reported by the experiment; for centre-of-mass motion or levitated particles, the total mass.
- **P6 non-classical state criteria (either suffices):** (i) Wigner-function negativity at ≥ 3σ; (ii) cat-state superposition separated in phase space by > 2× zero-point amplitude, evidenced by interference fringes or Wigner negativity. Near-ground-state cooling, squeezing, or Gaussian entanglement with everywhere-positive Wigner functions do not count.
- **P6.1 dual-channel disappearance criteria (both required):** (i) Inside the target's collective blind window (baseline 21.8 ± 0.3 μg), effective scattering cross-section or readout signal across all classical and non-classical channels drops to < 1% of its out-of-band baseline (or below noise floor); (ii) tuning m_eff or measuring control samples outside the window (< 21.5 μg or > 22.1 μg) immediately restores normal classical scattering and readout.
- **Observational Collective & environmental shift:** Because the target mass, probing light, vacuum, detector, observer's eye and body (frequency F_body = M_body c²/h), laboratory building, and surrounding heavy apparatus superpose in quadrature (v² = 2Φ) to form a collective frequency field, the blind threshold is a finite interval whose centre mass and width shift reproducibly across different laboratories, heavy-equipment states, and observer body-mass configurations.

## 3. Disclosure of data already examined
Experimental and observational datasets examined prior to or upon fixing these predictions:
- **P5:** Ciocan et al. 2026 (Astronomy and Astrophysics 709, L16) find a₀ rising with z, 2.38×10⁻¹⁰ at z ≈ 1. Direction agrees; magnitude about 19% above our prediction. Earlier analyses (Milgrom 2017; Limbach et al. 2008) disfavour strong evolution.
- **P3:** DESI DR2 BAO (13 points). Best χ² = 27.7 for our model (21.64 vs. 10.54 for ΛCDM with free r_d under identical degrees of freedom). This is unfavourable to our model, with the largest residual at z = 0.934.
- **P1:** current GW-only constraints (±15–20) do not discriminate.
- **P6 & P6.1:** Heaviest mechanical non-classical state to date is Bild et al. 2023 (Science 380, 274), cat states at m_eff = 16.2 μg (74% of the 21.8 μg gate); no high-resolution continuous mass-stepped coupling scan has yet been performed across 21.5–22.1 μg.

We will record every future comparison, favourable or not, without changing the predictions above.
