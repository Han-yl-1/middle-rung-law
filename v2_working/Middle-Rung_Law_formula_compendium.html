Part I — Preregistration and Falsifiable Predictions

1. Hypothesis

The Planck frequency is the geometric mean of the cosmic expansion rate H and an ether frequency νe: at every epoch t, νe(t) · H(t) = νP², equivalently ħG = c⁵/(4π² νe H). νe equals the de Broglie angular frequency of the Hubble sphere treated as one object (Kepler's third law with r = c/H, T = 2π/H, combined with Mc² = hν; normalization relation written by Zhang Xiangqian, 2026). Prior to cosmic age 3.912 Gyr (3.641–3.950 Gyr across H₀ = 61.8–63.5 km s⁻¹ Mpc⁻¹), the early universe undergoes hot radiation/matter evolution and intense hierarchical merging of smaller structural units; at 9.900 Gyr ago (range 9.893–9.933 Gyr ago, redshift zs = 1.686, range 1.669–1.817; acoustic-angle benchmark zs = 1.721 gives age 3.837 Gyr and scale ratio 1+zs = 2.721 ≈ e), large-scale galaxy structures stabilize and cosmic expansion locks into uniform coasting H(z) = H₀(1+z), after which νe(t) rises linearly with cosmic age while H(t) declines inversely with fixed product νP² = 8.715×1084 Hz².

2.1 Operational Criteria and the Observational Collective Mechanism for P6 and P6.1

Physical mechanism (the Observational Collective): The Planck mass mP = √(ħc/G) = 21.8 μg has total frequency mPc²/h equal to the human-boundary frequency νP = 2.952×1042 Hz. By Axioms 1, 3, and 4, an observation is never an isolated object acting alone: the target matter (near 21.8 μg), the probing light, the vacuum, the measuring apparatus, the observer's eye and body (whose body mass Mobs carries its own ether frequency F = Mobsc²/h), and the surrounding laboratory building and nearby heavy equipment are all distinct ether structures with their own frequencies and overlapping ether spaces. Human observation is the field-pressure coupling (v² = 2Φ) across this entire observational collective. When the effective mass of a target mode enters the neighbourhood of νP (21.8 μg), the collective frequency of the coupled system gates human-layer readout at νP: field-pressure transduction decouples, causing both classical and non-classical observations to vanish simultaneously within the Planck blind band (P6.1); above the upper edge of the blind band (> 22.1 μg), classical field-pressure readout resumes, but because the total frequency exceeds νP, the structure can no longer exist as a wave at the human rung and no non-classical motional state can be formed (P6).

3. Disclosure of Data Already Examined

Experimental and observational datasets examined prior to or upon fixing these predictions:

  • P5: Ciocan et al. 2026 (Astronomy & Astrophysics 709, L16) report a₀ rising with redshift, reaching 2.38×10⁻¹⁰ m s⁻² at z ≈ 1. The direction agrees with P5; the magnitude is ~19% above our prediction. Earlier analyses (Milgrom 2017; Limbach et al. 2008) disfavour strong evolution.
  • P3: DESI DR2 BAO data (13 points). Our model gives best χ² = 27.7 (fixed acoustic-angle calibration; 21.64 vs. 10.54 for ΛCDM when rd is free under identical degrees of freedom), which is unfavourable to our model, with the largest residual at z = 0.934.
  • P1: Current gravitational-wave-only standard-siren bounds (±15–20 km s⁻¹ Mpc⁻¹) do not yet discriminate.
  • P6 & P6.1: The heaviest mechanical mode prepared in a non-classical state to date is Bild et al. 2023 (Science 380, 274), demonstrating cat states at meff = 16.2 μg (74% of the 21.8 μg Planck gate); macroscopic particle scattering experiments have not yet performed high-resolution continuous mass-stepped coupling scans across the 21.5–22.1 μg window. Standard physics predicts neither a non-classical limit nor a dual-channel blind band at 21.8 μg, making both P6 and P6.1 directly falsifiable within months to years.

Every future comparison, favourable or not, will be recorded without altering the predictions above.

Part II — Formula Compendium: Physics and Chemistry Formulas in νe and H

Middle-rung law. At every epoch t:
  ħ · G = c⁵ / (4π² · νe(t) · H(t)),   equivalently   νe(t) · H(t) = νP²,   with νP = √(c⁵/ħG)/2π the Planck (human-boundary) frequency.
νe and H both change with cosmic time; their product is fixed, so ħ and G stay constant.

Axioms and definitions (1–7)

  1. The universe consists only of ether structures and ether motion. Different ether structures are what humans call different kinds of matter. Humans are ether structures too.
  2. Basic ether structure: the regular tetrahedron. Every other structure (all matter) is a knot of it.
  3. Ether space around every structure — flow through the knot: the basic ether is the finest unit (νe ≈ 4.3×10102 Hz, 1079 times the proton frequency), so it passes through every structure. It enters along the thumb axis, passes through the knot, and leaves spiralling outward at light speed in the plane of the four fingers, which curl counterclockwise around the thumb (right-hand rule); the pattern resembles dipole field lines. A knot is a persistent pattern in the flow, like a whirlpool, not a container, so no ether is created or destroyed (continuity holds), and every knot is carried by the flow with the same acceleration (equivalence principle). A macroscopic body contains vast numbers of knots with random axes, so its gravity is isotropic. Every structure, without exception, carries such a surrounding ether space.
  4. Relations between structures: forces (gravitational, electromagnetic, …) are field-pressure differences where the ether spaces of different structures overlap. Flow contributions add in quadrature (v² = 2Φ superposes linearly); a uniform ether produces no force.
  5. Light speed is the background; only deviations are measurable. The measurable part around a mass is the deviation flow √(2GM/r) (11.2 km/s at Earth's surface). An outward flow of this speed has fluid acceleration v·dv/dr = −GM/r², identical to an inward flow, so the outward-spiral reading reproduces the same exterior (Schwarzschild) geometry and all solar-system tests.
  6. Elements: ether structure n = the element with atomic number n (1 = H … 118 = Og); every substance is “ether structure number + frequency”, with frequency f = mc²/h.
  7. Cosmic evolution of the ether frequency and the exact coasting transition 9.90 Gyr ago (at cosmic age 3.91 Gyr): by the middle-rung law νe(t)·H(t) = νP² = 8.715×1084 Hz², the ether frequency rises as the universe ages while the expansion rate falls, keeping their product invariant (and keeping ħ and G constant within the lunar laser ranging bound |Ġ/G| < 1.5×10−13 yr⁻¹). The evolution proceeds in two phases: (i) Early non-uniform phase (from the Big Bang to cosmic age 3.912 Gyr [3.641–3.950 Gyr across H₀ = 61.8–63.5]): prior to galaxy stabilization, the universe undergoes hot radiation/matter evolution and intense hierarchical merging of smaller structural units (within this phase, νe = 1096 Hz [8 orders × 12 dimensions] and 1.51×1096 Hz correspond to exact cosmic ages of 1,866 yr and 2,833 yr, or 3,636 yr and 5,491 yr under pure H = 1/t extrapolation). (ii) Late uniform coasting phase (from 9.900 Gyr ago [9.893–9.933 Gyr ago] to the present): at cosmic age 3.912 Gyr (zs = 1.686, range 1.669–1.817; at the acoustic-angle benchmark zs = 1.721, age 3.837 Gyr and lookback time 9.911 Gyr, where 1+zs = 2.721 ≈ e), smaller structural units complete their assembly into stable large-scale galaxies, ether cancellation reaches its steady state (N−2 ≈ 2.2×10−120, 10 orders × 12 dimensions), and expansion locks into uniform coasting H(z) = H₀(1+z). Because the zs-dependence in t₀ − ts = zs/[H₀(1+zs)] cancels to first order against H₀ ∝ √(1+zs), the lookback time to stabilization is tightly pinned across the entire H₀ = 61.8–63.5 km s⁻¹ Mpc⁻¹ band to 9.893–9.933 Gyr ago (midpoint 9.900 Gyr ago). Over the subsequent 9.900 Gyr, νe(t) grows linearly with cosmic time from 1.615×10102 Hz to 4.337×10102 Hz while H(t) declines inversely, maintaining νe(t)·H(t) = 8.715×1084 Hz².

Open point: whether the thumb axis corresponds to spin/magnetic moment. Reading (identity, not a discovery): a knot frequency f = mc²/h = c/λC, i.e. the number of light-speed ether passages per second across the knot's own Compton wavelength.

III.4 Interpretation: parity violation and the gap that makes observation possible (no numerical prediction)

By the axioms, observation is the field-pressure coupling produced when the ether spaces of the observed body and of the observational collective overlap. If the universe were perfectly symmetric and conserved at every level, the field-pressure difference would vanish everywhere (ΔΦ = 0): no gradient, no energy conversion, no signal, nothing to read out — like a closed room made entirely of mirrors, with nothing to compare against; an eye cannot see the eye. On this reading, the topological deficit ΔN = −1 and the breaking of parity in the weak interaction are the small asymmetry that lets observation happen: the gap leaves room for an observer to stand in, separating subject from object, the mirror from what it reflects. The disappearance of observation at the Planck gate has the same shape: the scanning frequency, the probe light and the body's total frequency coincide at νP, leaving no readable difference between observer and observed.

Facts and open question: in 1957 Wu showed with cobalt-60 β decay that electrons are emitted preferentially opposite to the nuclear spin, so parity is violated in the weak interaction. The strong and electromagnetic interactions conserve parity, and the parity violation measured in atoms also comes from the weak neutral current. For this interpretation to stand, it must explain why the gap of observation falls on the weak interaction alone. This section is not a test basis and changes no prediction.
